# Real animal sound credits

These five clips are real recordings, all CC0 1.0 (public domain). CC0 does
not require attribution, but it's credited here anyway for provenance.

Derivative works by [DJ WoodZ](https://github.com/DJWoodZ/), CC0 1.0, from
the [Animal-Sounds](https://github.com/DJWoodZ/Animal-Sounds) project:

- `chicken-cluck.mp3` — derivative of "[Chicken Single Alarm Call](https://www.freesound.org/people/Rudmer_Rotteveel/sounds/316920/)" by Rudmer_Rotteveel (Freesound, CC0 1.0)
- `cow-moo.mp3` — derivative of "[Cow moos](http://freesound.org/people/josephsardin/sounds/177253/)" by josephsardin (Freesound, CC0 1.0)
- `horse-trot.mp3` — derivative of "[trotting horse in rural road.mp3](http://freesound.org/people/Oscar%20de%20%C3%81vila/sounds/139325/)" by Oscar de Ávila (Freesound, CC0 1.0)
- `pig-oink.mp3` — derivative of "[Pig - Multiple Snorts 3 (2 pigs) - 96kHz.wav](http://www.freesound.org/people/JarredGibb/sounds/233182/)" by JarredGibb (Freesound, CC0 1.0)
- `sheep-baa.mp3` — derivative of "[Flock of sheep pass by.wav](http://freesound.org/people/sandeepkurissery/sounds/245183/)" by sandeepkurissery (Freesound, CC0 1.0)

Giraffe, tiger, monkey, penguin, dog, cat, lion, duck, and elephant sounds
are synthesized in-browser (Web Audio API) — not recordings. They're marked
with a small 🎼 badge on their tile in the app.
